Snooker Scoreboard

Full 14.7 · tap a ball on the player you are scoring
Tap a ball in the top or bottom panel to score that player. Foul opens a 4–7 picker and awards the points to the other player. Free ball opens a 1–7 picker and adds those points to that player. Rotate lays the two players side by side. End visit closes the current break (used for statistics). After the final red, if the striker does not pot a colour (an End visit or Foul), the board moves straight to the final colour-potting sequence. Everything is saved in this browser and can be exported as JSON.
